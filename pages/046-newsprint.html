Locals Insist Morning Coffee Improves Mood; Study Conducted Only After Second Cup

By A. Sipping, Correspondent

The municipal caffeine board reported today that 94% of respondents felt "significantly more vertical" after regular consumption of roasted bean water. Dissenting voices — three, all decaf drinkers — were politely asked to revisit their position.

Side effects announced at the press conference included dreaming in brown and an unshakeable opinion about grinders.

Meanwhile, the city's oldest percolator, "Old Reliable," marked its 41st anniversary in continuous service. Officials presented it with a plaque; it responded by overflowing, which witnesses described as "classic."

Experts advise hydration between cups. Experts also advise most things between cups.
