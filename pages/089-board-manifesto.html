Manifesto of the Small Table

  1. Article IEvery gathering deserves a table too small for its ambitions.
  2. Article IICandles are mandatory; competence is optional. Wax in the pasta is seasoning.
  3. Article IIINo phones above the bread plate. The bread plate is sacred.
  4. Article IVSomeone must cook, someone must wash, one person may dramatically peel garlic and call it contribution.
  5. Article VDessert happens regardless of dinner quality. Solidarity sugar.
“We do not gather to be impressive. We gather because impressive people eat alone and own nice dishes nobody sees.”

ratified unanimously by six friends, one partner's colleague, and a cat with veto power.
