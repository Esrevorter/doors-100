Polystichum setiferum — Soft Shield Fern

In the damp hollows behind the mill, the soft shield fern unfurls its fronds like letters no one rushed to send. Each pinna finely serrated; each crozier coiled with the patience of a question.

Collectors prize it, deer ignore it, poets exaggerate it. Spores mature by August; dispersal occurs entirely on the whims of air.
