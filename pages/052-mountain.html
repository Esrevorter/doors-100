Day 41 · 2,910m

The marmots unionised

Seven whistles before dawn, coordinated, smug. The glacier above them hasn't moved in forty years and neither, apparently, have their grievances.

Day 47 · 3,340m

Weather turned silver

Came up blue, went away white. Ate lunch facing the tent wall like a coward. The wind practised scales on the pitons all night — mostly minor keys.

Day 52 · 3,842m · SUMMIT

Quiet, finally

At the top, weather is underneath you. It does something to the brain. I stood eleven minutes, said nothing, meant everything.
