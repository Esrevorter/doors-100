THE WAY OF TEA

一
Warm the room, not just the cup. Host and guest agree, without saying, to slow down together.
二
Water at 80°, never boiling. Green leaves are shy; scalding them makes them bitter about it.
三
Pour in circles. A steady ring, like stone skipping on water, blends each infusion evenly.
四
Drink in three sips. First for heat, second for taste, third for whatever the taste was trying to say.
ichi-go ich-e — one time, one meeting. This cup will never be this cup again.
