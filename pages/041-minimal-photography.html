Light Leaks

Twelve frames shot on expired film, one camera held badly.

Lisbon, golden hour, metering wrong on purpose
Harbour fog. The ferry arrived late and beautiful.
Forest path, ISO pushed, grain like pollen
Neon alley, lens flare bleeding through the shutter

I keep the mistakes. Every leak is proof the camera was alive, that light found a way in where it wasn't invited. Ask me nicely and I'll sell you frame four — the one where everything is overexposed except the dog.
