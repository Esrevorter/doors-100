Letters to No One

prose poems by the night shift

I. Inventory

I keep the sounds I can't return: a screen door confessing to summer, the bus sighing like it's seen everything. My pockets are a museum of almost-goodbyes.

II. Weather Report

Low pressure moving through the kitchen. Chance of reheated coffee, certain of staring. Somewhere a kettle commits to its ambition.

III. Instruction Manual for a Chair

Hold still while someone decides whether to sit. Be sturdy in the ways nobody thanks you for. When they leave, keep the warmth a little longer. That's the job.
