Herbarium
Nocturne

Moonflower

Ipomoea alba

Unfurls only after dusk, chasing the moon with white satin trumpets. Farmers once read time by its opening.

Queen of the Night

Epiphyllum oxypetalum

Blooms once a year, for a single night, then vanishes before dawn. Gardens throw parties around its schedule.

Dame's Rocket

Hesperis matronalis

Releases its scent at twilight when day-pollinators sleep. A letter written to moths.
