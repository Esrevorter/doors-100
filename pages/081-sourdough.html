The Levain Log

batch journal of a kitchen learning patience from yeast

78°

Week 12 — “The Comeback”

Starter doubled in four hours, aggressive, joyful. Bulk went long (my phone, my fault). Crust sang during cooling — literally, crackling like tiny applause. Ear opened perfectly. Not bragging. Bragging slightly.

71°

Week 11 — “Cold Proof, Cold Heart”

Fridge at 4°C, dough unimpressed, proofed like it owed money somewhere. Dense crumb. Tasted of lesson learned: the fridge is a pause button, not a friend.

80°

Week 10 — “Overproof Autopsy”

Left it ninety minutes too long. Slack, blobby, dignified no more. Baked anyway; ate anyway; forgiveness is a flatbread.
